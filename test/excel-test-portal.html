Exam Scenario: Semester Result Management

Welcome to your Module 4 Practical Assessment. You have been assigned the role of a Class Administrator. Your objective is to build a professional "Semester Result Sheet" for 5 students. This test evaluates your ability to structure data, implement logical formulas, use absolute referencing, and prepare professional data visualizations. Follow the tasks below in sequence.

Task 1

Data Entry & Formatting

10 Marks

Begin by setting up the foundation of your workbook. Proper formatting ensures readability for stakeholders.

  • Rename the active sheet to Student_Marksheet.
  • Create headers: Roll No, Name, Maths, Science, English, Total, Average, Result, Final Grade.
  • Enter data for 5 students (Marks: 0-100).
  • Apply Bold headers, background colors, and All Borders.
Task 2

Logic & Statistics

15 Marks

Apply mathematical functions to calculate outcomes. Ensure your IF logic correctly identifies passing students.

Total: SUM of all 3 subjects.
Average: Arithmetic mean.
Result: IF Average >= 50, "Pass"; else "Fail".
Stats: MAX, MIN, COUNT for Science.
Task 3

Absolute Referencing

5 Marks

Critical Skill: Use a fixed cell reference for common values that apply to all rows.

Place "Grace Marks" in cell K1 and the value 5 in L1.

Calculation:
Adjusted Total = Total + $L$1
